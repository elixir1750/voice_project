# Thesis (ICASSP-style draft)

- `main.tex` — English paper draft. Compiles with a stock TeX Live
  (`pdflatex main.tex` twice). To submit, swap the preamble for the official
  ICASSP `spconf.sty` template as noted at the top of `main.tex`.
- `main.pdf` — compiled 4-page draft.
- `figures/` — figure PDFs and `make_figures.py` that regenerates them from the
  numbers in `results/`.
